# 📚 SmartInv Study Guide

> A comprehensive guide for understanding the SmartInv research paper (IEEE S&P 2024) by Columbia University.

## Paper
**SmartInv: Multimodal Learning for Smart Contract Invariant Inference**  
Authors: Sally Junsong Wang, Kexin Pei, Junfeng Yang  
Published: IEEE Symposium on Security & Privacy 2024  
GitHub: https://github.com/columbia/SmartInv

## Guide Structure

| File | Topic | Read Time |
|---|---|---|
| [Part 1](Part_1_The_Big_Picture.md) | **The Big Picture** — Problem, motivation, what SmartInv is | ~10 min |

## How to Study
1. Read Part 1 first → get the big picture
2. Skim Part 2 → use as a dictionary for unfamiliar terms
3. Study Part 3 deeply → this is the heart of the paper
4. Memorize key numbers from Part 4 → 3.5×, 4×, 150×, 119 zero-days
5. Practice Part 5 out loud → rehearse Q&As like a mock interview
